    Five moves of the go-to AI person

    1Solve real problems for colleagues

    "Here's how I'd handle your quarterly report" beats "here are 10 AI tips."

    2Translate

    Explain AI in the language of the job: claims, renewals, contracts, pipeline. No model jargon.

    3Be honest about limits

    "Don't use it for this, here's why" earns trust hype never does.

    4Make wins visible and reusable

    Share the template, the before-and-after, and the time saved, so others can copy it.

    5Filter the noise

    Colleagues don't track every release, because you tell them what matters for their work.

    Build · Live · Weekly Leadership Update

    Let Claude interview you, then write the Skill

    Help me build a Claude Skill for my weekly leadership update. Interview me one question at a time about: who reads it and when, what I'll paste in (notes, tracker exports, Slack threads), the exact output format, constraints, one example of a great update, how my director judges it, and what it must never do. Then write the Skill with a clear name and a description of when to use it, so I can save it.

    Build · What Claude writes · Example

    The Skill, in plain English

    weekly-leadership-updateUse when I paste weekly notes and ask for my leadership update
    Context

    My director reads it Monday at 8am, on a phone.

    Format

    5 bullets: win · progress vs plan · risk + fix · decision needed · next week.

    Constraints

    Under 120 words. Numbers only from my notes. No "great" or "amazing."

    Judged by

    Could my director act on it in 60 seconds?

    Never

    Invent names, dates, or numbers. Mark anything missing as [CHECK]. Leave out HR details.

    Example

    One real update I was proud of, pasted in full.

    Example content for the demo. Yours comes from your interview answers.

    Build · Make it visible

    A win nobody sees doesn't count

    • Share the Skill with the two colleagues who write the same update.
    • Publish to org once it's proven, so anyone can install it.
    • Post the before-and-after in your team channel, in the language of the job.

I've shared the Skill with you. Use it for your weekly update. Don't use it for anything with HR details. Happy to set it up with you.
